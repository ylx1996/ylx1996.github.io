Dr. Yang founded **[AIxLab (艾克斯实验室)](https://snnuaixlab.github.io/aixlab/)** at Shaanxi Normal University. The lab focuses on AI security, including the security and trustworthiness of large language models, multimodal models, and AI agents. Its research also covers trusted data interaction and blockchain scalability and security. The name stands for *Artificial Intelligence eXtended*, with X representing multimodality, exploration of the unknown, and future intelligence.

Together with undergraduate students from SNNU, Dr. Yang also established the **"Academic Bridge" research group**. The group focuses primarily on AI, including computer vision (CV), natural language processing (NLP), and multimodal learning. Its research also covers Web3, blockchain, and cybersecurity.
