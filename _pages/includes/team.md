Dr. Yang founded **AIxLab (艾克斯实验室)**. The name stands for *Artificial Intelligence eXtended*, with X representing multimodality, exploration of the unknown, and future intelligence.

Together with undergraduate students from SNNU, Dr. Yang also established the **"Academic Bridge" research group**. The group focuses primarily on AI, including computer vision (CV), natural language processing (NLP), and multimodal learning. Its research also covers Web3, blockchain, and cybersecurity.
